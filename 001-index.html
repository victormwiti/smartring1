The Problem

Smartphones Were Not
Built for Everyone

2 in 5 people with visual impairment struggle to use standard touchscreen phones. Existing solutions are either passive, bulky, or cost-prohibitive — leaving a massive underserved population without digital access.

Touch Barrier

Standard smartphones require precise visual feedback and gesture accuracy that is often impossible for visually impaired users.

Cost Exclusion

Most dedicated braille and navigation devices retail for over $500, pricing out the majority of users — especially in developing markets.

Passive, Not Empowering

Current assistive tech reads the world to users; it doesn't let them actively control their digital environment on their own terms.

Growing Crisis

An estimated 385 million people globally will live with blindness or severe visual impairment by 2030 — and digital exclusion deepens that inequality.

The Solution

Meet the Smart Ring

A sleek, wearable Bluetooth ring with 4 programmable buttons and a touch pad. It wirelessly connects to any smartphone and allows full OS navigation through simple, tactile gestures — no screen needed.

🔵
Bluetooth Connectivity

Seamlessly pairs with any smartphone via Bluetooth. Ring → Bluetooth → Smartphone navigation in seconds.

👆
4 Buttons + Touch Pad

Physical buttons provide tactile certainty. The touch pad adds swipe navigation — no visual feedback required.

📳
Haptic Feedback

Vibration profiles confirm every action — scrolling, selecting, calling. Customisable for different apps and workflows.

💍
Always With You

Worn on the finger — it can't be misplaced like a cane or headset. The most personal assistive device possible.

⚡
ESP32-S3 Powered

Built on high-performance, low-cost ESP32-S3 hardware. Delivers premium capability at an accessible price point.

🌍
Designed for All Markets

3D-printed casing keeps unit cost near $20, enabling distribution in developing markets where need is greatest.
